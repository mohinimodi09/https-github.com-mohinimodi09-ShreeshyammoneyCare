Financial Restatement

We prepare certified restated financial statements that meet the stringent requirements of SEBI, stock exchanges, and institutional investors. Whether you are planning an IPO, raising funds, or complying with regulatory mandates, our restatement services ensure accuracy, consistency, and full regulatory alignment.

Historical Financial Restatement

Recompilation and restatement of past financial statements to comply with Ind AS, IFRS, or Schedule III formats.

Audit & Certification

Independent audit and certification of restated financials by qualified Chartered Accountants for regulatory acceptance.

IPO Compliance Formatting

Preparation of financials in the exact format required by SEBI and stock exchanges for DRHP and RHP filings.

Adjustment & Reconciliation

Identification and quantification of material adjustments needed between original and restated figures.

Accounting Standard Alignment

Restatement to align with current accounting policies, estimates, and applicable accounting standards consistently.

Regulatory Review Support
